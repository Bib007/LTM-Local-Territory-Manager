# Local setup and owner checks

[Dansk](Dette-skal-hjaelpes-manuelt.md)

Install stable Distrikt from the repository through Home Assistant, then configure your own private address and app password locally. For beta testing, follow the [separate beta guide](docs/BETA.md). Keep real credentials and connection files out of feedback.

Before migrating an existing local installation, take a private backup and stop the old app. Keep it stopped until the new installation has been checked. Follow the [installation guide](docs/INSTALL.en.md).

The fixed development plan does not include performing these checks on your behalf:

- Print an A4 calibration sheet and the actual front/back stock; check alignment and QR readability.
- Install/update beta on your own HA, phone and VPN; check login and backup/restore using your own records locally.
- Approve your own Google OAuth connection and check published access, return/reassignment and QR links from your own phone.
- Verify the configured Datafordeler account and address refresh on your own installation.
- Check assistive technology on your own device if used.

See [final verification](docs/SLUTKONTROL.en.md), [printing](docs/PRINT.en.md) and [Google Drive](docs/GOOGLE-DRIVE.en.md). These remaining local checks do not authorize publication of private data.
