# LTM 1.12.10 · Correct completion history and report periods

Remove an incorrectly registered completion after reviewing the exact card, date and related assignment. Removing it preserves an active assignment and recalculates the card's latest completion without allowing the deleted date to return through later history.

Reports now show one selected period at a time. Choose a calendar year, service year or the last 12 months to see the matching totals, chart and map. Calendar-year selection is shown only when it applies.

Update normally in Home Assistant, preserving the installation and data. Keep private data/account backups. Do not uninstall or remove data.
